FAQs

Can AI automate my entire business?

Not entirely, but it can automate 60-80% of repetitive tasks. Customer onboarding, data entry, email follow-ups, report generation, and social media posting are all easily automated. Strategy, creative work, and relationship-building still need humans.

What's the difference between RPA and AI automation?

RPA (Robotic Process Automation) mimics human actions—clicking buttons, copying data. AI automation understands context, makes decisions, and handles exceptions. Tools like UiPath combine both. For most small businesses, AI automation platforms like Zapier or Make are more practical than RPA.

Is no-code automation secure?

Yes, when using reputable platforms. Zapier, Make, and n8n all use encrypted connections, OAuth for API access (no stored passwords), and audit logs. For sensitive data, self-hosted options like n8n give you full control.
