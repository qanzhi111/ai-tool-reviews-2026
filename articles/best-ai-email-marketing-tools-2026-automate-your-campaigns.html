3. HubSpot Email Marketing — Best for CRM Integration

HubSpot's email marketing is deeply integrated with their CRM, giving you a complete view of every contact's journey. FAQs

Can AI write email campaigns automatically?

Yes. Tools like GetResponse and HubSpot can generate complete email campaigns from a brief description. The AI writes subject lines, body copy, and CTAs—but you should always review and add your personal touch before sending.

What's the best free AI email marketing tool?

MailerLite offers the most generous free plan (1,000 subscribers with full automation). Brevo is great if you send fewer emails (300/day free). Mailchimp's free plan includes AI Content Optimizer.

Does AI improve email open rates?

Absolutely. AI-powered send time optimization alone can increase open rates by 20-30%. Subject line optimization adds another 10-15%. Combine both, and you're looking at significantly better performance with zero extra effort.

Final Verdict

For most businesses, Mailchimp remains the best all-around AI email marketing tool. If you're in e-commerce, Klaviyo is the clear winner. And if you want the most powerful automation, ActiveCampaign delivers.

Start with a free plan, test the AI features that matter most to you, and upgrade when you see results. Your subscribers—and your revenue—will thank you.
