1. Thinkific — Best for Full Control Over Your Course Business

Thinkific gives course creators the most granular control over their student experience, pricing, and branding. Their free plan is one of the most generous in the industry.

AI Highlights:

  • AI Course Outline Generator: Creates structured curriculum from a topic description
  • AI Quiz Builder: Automatically generates quizzes based on lesson content
  • AI Student Progress Insights: Predicts which students might need additional support

Pros: Free plan supports 1 course with full features; Excellent student management and progress tracking; No transaction fees on paid plans; Strong community and learning resources; 30% recurring commission affiliate program.

Cons: Design customization limited on free plan; Built-in email marketing is basic.

Best for: Creators who want professional course delivery without platform fees eating into revenue.

2. Kajabi — Best All-in-One Knowledge Business Platform

Kajabi is the premium choice for creators who want everything — courses, website, email marketing, community, coaching, and payments — in one platform. It's the "Apple ecosystem" of course platforms.

AI Highlights:

  • AI Course Builder: Generates complete course outlines and lesson content
  • AI Email Assistant: Writes marketing emails and sequences
  • AI Landing Page Copy: Creates conversion-optimized page copy
  • AI Community Prompts: Suggests engagement topics for community spaces

Pros: True all-in-one eliminates need for separate tools; Professional templates with excellent design; Built-in podcast hosting and community features; 30% recurring commission affiliate program.

Cons: Most expensive option ($149/month minimum); Learning curve due to feature depth.

Pricing: 14-day free trial. Basic from $149/month.

Best for: Serious creators who want to run their entire business on one platform and can justify the premium price.

3. Teachable — Best for Simple, Fast Course Launches

Teachable is built for speed. If you want to record a course, set a price, and start selling within a day, Teachable gets you there with minimal friction.

AI Highlights:

  • AI Curriculum Generator: Creates course structure from a brief description
  • Teachable AI Copilot: Assists with course content creation and student communication
  • AI Sales Page Builder: Generates copy for course sales pages

Pros: Extremely fast setup; Clean, modern student experience; Excellent payment processing with built-in affiliate system; Strong mobile app for students.

Cons: Transaction fees on free and basic plans (5% + payment processing); Limited community features.

Pricing: Free plan available (with transaction fees). Basic from $39/month.

Best for: Creators who want to launch quickly without a steep learning curve.

4. Podia — Best for Selling Digital Products Beyond Courses

Podia excels when your business isn't just courses — it's also ebooks, templates, coaching sessions, and community access. Their bundling and upselling features are best-in-class.

AI Highlights:

  • AI Course Outline: Generates course structure from topics
  • AI Email Writer: Creates marketing and announcement emails
  • AI Product Descriptions: Writes compelling copy for digital products

Pros: Sell courses, downloads, coaching, and community in one place; Excellent bundling and upselling tools; No transaction fees on any plan.

Cons: Course features less sophisticated than Thinkific or Kajabi; Limited assessment options.

Pricing: 14-day free trial. Starter from $39/month.

Best for: Creators with diverse digital product offerings who want everything in one storefront.

5. LearnWorlds — Best for Interactive Learning Experiences

LearnWorlds is the choice for educators who want truly interactive learning — think embedded coding environments, interactive videos, and social learning features that go beyond basic video + quiz.

AI Highlights:

  • AI Course Assistant: Helps build and structure course content
  • AI Assessment Builder: Creates varied assessment types automatically
  • AI Interactive Video: Adds interactive elements to video content

Pros: Most interactive learning features available; Excellent for corporate training; Built-in social learning and community; SCORM compliance.

Cons: Higher learning curve than simpler platforms; Pricing can escalate with add-ons.

Pricing: 30-day free trial. Starter from $29/month.

Best for: Professional educators and corporate trainers who need interactive, engaging learning experiences.

6. Udemy — Best for Marketplace Reach (But Lower Revenue)

Udemy operates differently from the other platforms — it's a marketplace, not a standalone platform. You get access to millions of potential students, but at the cost of much lower revenue share and less control.

AI Highlights:

  • AI Course Structure Suggestions: Recommends curriculum based on market demand
  • AI Market Insights: Shows demand and competition data for course topics

Pros: Instant access to 60+ million students; No upfront cost to publish; Built-in marketing and discovery.

Cons: Only 37% revenue share on organic sales; No control over pricing; Cannot collect student email addresses.

Pricing: Free to publish. Revenue share model.

Best for: New creators who want to validate course ideas and reach a large audience before investing in their own platform.

How to Choose Your Online Course Platform

  • For maximum control and no fees: Thinkific's free plan is hard to beat.
  • For all-in-one business operations: Kajabi justifies its premium price by replacing 5+ separate tools.
  • For fastest launch: Teachable gets you selling in a single afternoon.
  • For diverse digital products: Podia handles courses, downloads, coaching, and community together.
  • For interactive education: LearnWorlds creates the most engaging student experience.
  • For marketplace discovery: Udemy offers reach, but don't build your business on rented land.

Pro Tip: Start Free, Then Migrate

Most successful course creators follow this path:

  1. Validate your course idea on Udemy or with a free Thinkific/Teachable tier
  2. Build your audience using the free plan
  3. Upgrade to a paid plan when revenue justifies it
  4. Scale with Kajabi or a premium Thinkific plan when you need the full marketing stack

The key mistake to avoid: over-investing in platform features before you've validated that students will pay for your course.

The Bottom Line

The best online course platform in 2026 is the one that matches your current stage, not the one with the most features. Start simple, validate demand, and upgrade when the revenue supports it. Every platform on this list has a free trial or free tier — there's no reason not to start today.
