Recurring commission (also called lifetime commission or ongoing commission) means you get paid a percentage every month your referred customer remains a subscriber. Unlike one-time commissions where you earn once and it's over, recurring commissions compound over time. **The math is simple but powerful:** Refer 10 customers to a $50/month tool at 30% commission = $150/month passive income. After 12 months, that's $1,800 from a single batch of referrals.
