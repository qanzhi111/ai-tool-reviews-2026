Superhuman has redefined what an email client can be. Known for its lightning-fast interface and AI-powered features, it's the preferred choice for professionals who treat email as their primary communication channel.

Key Features

  • Instant Preview: See email content without opening messages
  • Split Inbox: Automatically categorize emails into "Focused" and "Other"
  • AI Compose: Generate email responses with a single keystroke
  • Undo Send: 30-second window to recall any sent email
  • Read Receipts: Track when your emails are opened

Pros

  • 🚀 Blazing fast performance (3x faster than traditional clients)
  • 🎯 Intuitive keyboard shortcuts reduce mouse usage by 90%
  • 📊 Built-in analytics dashboard
  • 💼 Excellent integration with calendar and task management

Cons

  • 💰 Premium pricing ($30/month)
  • 📧 Only available for Gmail and Outlook
  • 📱 No mobile app (desktop only)

Key Features

  • Email Scoring: Real-time feedback on email effectiveness
  • Personalization AI: Suggests personalized opening lines based on recipient data
  • Social Data: Integrates LinkedIn and company information
  • Team Analytics: Track team-wide email performance
  • Template Library: 100+ proven email templates

Pros

  • 📈 Proven to increase reply rates by 25%
  • 🔄 Seamless CRM integration
  • 👥 Excellent for team collaboration
  • 📧 Works across Gmail, Outlook, and LinkedIn

Cons

  • ⚙️ Requires setup time to optimize
  • 🎯 Primarily focused on sales outreach
  • 📊 Advanced analytics require higher-tier plans

Pricing

Lavender offers a free Chrome extension with basic features. Paid plans start at $59/month per user for full access.

  • AI Writing Assistant: Draft and improve email content
  • Slack Integration: Sync team communications

Pros

  • 📋 Excellent task management features

Cons

How to Choose the Right AI Email Assistant

With so many options available, selecting the right AI email assistant depends on your specific needs:

Consider These Factors:

  1. Primary Use Case: Sales teams benefit from Lavender, while collaborative teams prefer Missive.
  2. Budget: Free options like Shortwave and Lavender's Chrome extension offer great value.
  3. Email Provider: Some tools only work with Gmail or Outlook.
  4. Team Size: Collaboration features matter more for teams.
  5. Security Needs: Canary Mail offers the strongest encryption.
