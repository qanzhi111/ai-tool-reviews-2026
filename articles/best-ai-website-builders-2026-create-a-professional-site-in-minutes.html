FAQs

Can AI build a website from scratch?

Yes! Tools like Wix, Hostinger, and Durable generate complete websites from a text description. You describe your business, and the AI creates a full design with pages, images, and copy.

Are AI-built websites good for SEO?

Most AI website builders include basic SEO features (meta tags, sitemaps, mobile optimization). For advanced SEO, pair your builder with an AI SEO tool like Surfer SEO or Semrush.

Is an AI website builder better than hiring a developer?

For simple to medium-complexity sites, AI builders save thousands of dollars and weeks of time. For highly custom applications, a developer is still the way to go. Many businesses start with an AI builder and hire a developer when they need advanced features.
